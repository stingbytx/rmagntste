About Remy Agent A new way to talk with AI.

Natural conversations. Real-time intelligence. Multiple AI models. Remy Agent brings voice and artificial intelligence together so people can talk with AI instead of typing at it.

Our story

A simpler way to reach AI

Remy Agent was created with a simple idea: interacting with artificial intelligence should feel natural, accessible and effortless.

Most AI tools ask you to type. We wanted a more human way of communicating — one that lets you speak, listen and think in the same rhythm as a real conversation. That idea became Remy Agent: a real-time AI voice platform where you can talk with AI and explore several models through one experience.

Our mission

Making AI voice interaction accessible to everyone.

We believe voice makes artificial intelligence easier to approach and more useful in everyday life. Our mission is to make natural AI conversations available to people around the world through a simple, intuitive experience — in the browser, in the language they prefer.

What makes Remy different

Multiple AI models

Bring multiple AI models into one voice experience, giving you access to different AI capabilities in a single place.

Real-time voice conversations

Interact with AI through voice instead of relying only on traditional text-based chat.

Multilingual accessibility

Make AI voice interaction easier to reach for people from different language backgrounds.

Simplicity

An approachable experience that makes starting an AI conversation straightforward.
