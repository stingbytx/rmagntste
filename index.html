What is Remy Agent

Remy Agent is a free, browser-based AI voice assistant. You speak, and Remy answers out loud — in real time, in the language you are comfortable with, using multiple AI models.

Talk Naturally with AI

Skip the typing. Ask a question, think out loud or work through an idea, and Remy replies in a natural conversational flow.

Multiple AI Models, One Voice Assistant

Different questions suit different models. Remy brings several AI models into a single voice experience, so you can explore a range of capabilities in one place.

Real-Time AI Voice Conversations

Remy listens as you speak and responds in the moment, so the conversation keeps moving. Open the web app, allow microphone access and start talking.

Multilingual AI Conversations

Speak in the language you are most comfortable with. Remy is built for multilingual voice interaction, making AI easier to reach for people around the world.

Why Choose Remy Agent?

  • Free to start. Open the web app and try a real voice conversation.
  • Runs in your browser. No download, on desktop and mobile.
  • Multiple AI models. One place to explore different AI capabilities.
  • Voice first. A more natural way to ask, explore and learn than typing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Remy Agent?

Remy Agent is a web-based AI voice assistant. You speak to it in your browser and it answers out loud in real time, drawing on multiple AI models.

Is Remy Agent free to use?

Yes. You can open the web app and start a voice conversation free of charge.

Can I choose different AI models?

Remy brings multiple AI models into one voice experience, so you can explore different capabilities from a single place.

Does Remy Agent support multiple languages?

Yes. Remy is built for multilingual conversations — speak in the language you are most comfortable with.

Can I use Remy Agent on mobile?

Yes. Remy runs in a modern mobile browser, so you can start a conversation without installing an app.

Does Remy Agent store voice conversations?

Voice is processed so Remy can respond in real time. What is processed, and how long it is kept, is described in the Privacy Policy.

How do I start a conversation?

Select “Launch Remy” to open chat.remyagent.cc, allow microphone access when your browser asks, and begin speaking.
