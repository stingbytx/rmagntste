Remy Agent Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Who we are

Remy Agent is an AI voice chat product published by DotcomAI, an AI-focused technology initiative under Dotcom One. This policy covers this website at remyagent.cc and the Remy Agent web app at chat.remyagent.cc.

2. Information we handle

This website

This website is a static, informational site. It does not offer accounts, sign-up forms or payments, and it does not set advertising cookies. Our hosting provider may record standard technical information — such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested — in server logs for security and reliability.

The voice app

When you use the Remy Agent web app, your browser asks for microphone access. If you allow it, audio from your microphone is processed so the AI can understand you and respond. Text of your requests and the AI responses may be processed by the AI model providers that generate the answer.

Please do not say anything you would not want processed by a third-party AI service.

3. How information is used

  • to provide the voice conversation and generate responses;
  • to keep the service secure, reliable and working correctly;
  • to understand and fix technical problems;
  • to respond to you when you contact us.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that host the site, operate the AI models or help us run the service. They are only given what is needed to perform those tasks.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The web app may store small preferences — such as your selected model or settings — in your browser’s local storage so the experience stays consistent between visits. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

6. Retention

Information is kept only for as long as it is needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. Server logs are kept for a limited period. If you would like to know what is retained about you, contact us.

7. Your choices and rights

You can decline or withdraw microphone permission in your browser at any time. You may also ask us about the information we hold about you, request a copy, or request that it be deleted. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data protection law.

To make a request, email help@remyagent.cc.

8. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The current version is always published on this page, with the date shown above.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: help@remyagent.cc.

Remy Agent is published by DotcomAI under Dotcom One.
